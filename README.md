## Selected work at Studio Tim Fu

### STF Finance

I built workflows for extracting invoice data from uploaded documents, importing Wise bank statements, and reconciling payments against invoices. The reconciliation handles partial payments and multiple currencies. I also worked on the client portal for viewing invoices and downloading PDFs.

### STF Studio

I rebuilt the studio’s internal operations app in Next.js. It brings project schedules, staff leave approvals, and the content calendar into one place. I integrated approved leave with Google Calendar and built reporting for the finance team.

### STF Hub for Windows

I integrated the Electron desktop app with the Studio Hub accounts and project data. Staff can see what they’re assigned to and update their status from a tray widget. I also worked on the Windows installer, in-app updates, and idle CPU usage.

### Client Review Portal

I turned a design prototype into a working portal for architectural reviews. Clients can pin feedback directly to the relevant drawing or render, and the studio can track revisions and approvals separately for each submission.

### STF Ideaboard

I built out a mobile-first workspace where the team saves visual references, develops ideas, and tracks how published posts perform. I implemented live board updates with Server-Sent Events, along with social imports and video previews.
